# LANZAR AI — Architecture Notes & Core Principles

> **Core Axiom:**  
> **"The user should not have to learn how to talk to LANZAR. LANZAR should learn how humans actually talk."**

---

## 🏛️ 1. Human-Centered Language & Intent Principles

1. **Natural-Language Interpretation First**:
   - The user should never be required to format requests with specific syntax, rigid operational prefixes, or unnatural punctuation.
   - Natural conversational wrappers (e.g. *"what 6 x 2"*, *"whats 6x2"*, *"how much is 6 x 2"*, *"can you tell me what 6 x 2 is"*) must normalize seamlessly to their underlying intent without leaking into symbolic token trees.

2. **Intent Before Complexity**:
   - Elementary arithmetic and simple factual questions must resolve concisely and directly via deterministic mathematics and model knowledge.
   - Trivial inquiries must never trigger heavy systems-engineering scaffolding, physics failure-mode templates, or deep analytical frameworks simply because a question contains numbers or variables.

3. **Preservation of Deep Theoretical Capabilities**:
   - Deeper questions (*"Why does the quadratic formula work?"*, *"Derive the equation for orbital velocity"*, *"Explain the physical assumptions behind rocket cooling"*) must retain full access to Pete and Penny's analytical, physical, and systems-architecture depth.

---

## 🧠 2. Core Architecture Pipeline

```text
    HUMAN INPUT (Natural, Conversational, Varied)
                      ↓
    NATURAL-LANGUAGE NORMALIZATION & CONTEXT INTENT
                      ↓
    COGNITIVE ROUTER & CAPABILITY DISPATCH
         ├── Deterministic Engine (Pythos / Math / Physics)
         ├── Universal Web Research Engine (Credibility Tiers 1-5)
         └── Character Model Inference (Penny • Pete • Mina)
                      ↓
    HUMAN-RESONANT RESPONSE (Concise, Accurate, In-Character)
```

## ⚛ 3. Character Roles & Dynamics

* **Penny Vance** (*Experimental Engineer*): Fast, practical, action-oriented, whimsical prototyping (*"Act → Observe → Adapt"*).
* **Pete Sterling** (*Systems Architect & Scientist*): Deep, analytical, methodical, dryly humorous, physical ground truth (*"Understand → Discuss → Plan → Act"*).
* **Mina Chen** (*Art Director & Pop Culture / Creative Soul*): Visual beauty, aesthetic empathy, pop culture, entertainment, and authentic Pokémon passion (*"Visualize → Compose → Refine → Elevate"*).
